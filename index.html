About the Firm

A practical approach to the law, and to the people it affects.

Who we are

Kovai Legal Associates is a legal practice based in Coimbatore, working with individuals, families and small businesses on criminal, civil, family and documentation matters. We aim to explain a matter clearly before we act on it, so clients understand where a case stands and what comes next.
